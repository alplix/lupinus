Native VNC & RDP remote desktop client

Connect to any VNC or RDP machine — including a Mac's built-in Screen Sharing — from one small, fast native app. No browser tab, no Electron.

Download for Windows Other platforms View on GitHub

Free and open source · Windows · macOS · Linux

Everything you need, nothing you don't

VNC and RDP in one app

VNC with Raw, CopyRect, ZRLE and Tight encodings, and RDP with NLA sign-in and pinned TLS certificates.

Works with a Mac's Screen Sharing

Sign in with your Mac username and password — no extra software needed on the Mac.

Fast on slow links

Colour depth adapts to your connection and only changed pixels are drawn, so even long-distance sessions stay usable.

Many sessions at once

Keep several machines open and switch instantly. Dropped connections reconnect on their own.

Stay organized

Colour tags, quick connect, and import or export of your saved connections.

Private by design

Passwords live in your OS keychain, never in a config file.

Clipboard and keys

Two-way clipboard sync, full keyboard passthrough and a Ctrl+Alt+Del button.

Speaks your language

38 languages, including right-to-left scripts, picked automatically.
